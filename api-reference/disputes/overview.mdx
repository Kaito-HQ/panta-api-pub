---
title: Dispute a proposal
description: File a dispute after the oracle proposes an outcome and before that proposal is finalized.
---

The API builds an unsigned transaction. The wallet signs it. You broadcast it. The same transaction files the dispute and pays the USDC bond.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build">
    [`POST /markets/{marketId}/dispute/build/`](/api-reference/disputes/build) — checks the window, the share balance, and the side, then returns instructions and a `disputeId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and broadcast">
    Compile a versioned transaction from `instructions` and `recentBlockhash`. Sign with `wallet`. Send on your RPC. Panta does not broadcast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    [`POST /markets/{marketId}/dispute/`](/api-reference/disputes/submit) after the signature is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status">
    [`GET /markets/{marketId}/dispute/?wallet=`](/api-reference/disputes/status) — this account's dispute for that wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The wallet must currently hold YES or NO shares. A wallet with zero shares receives `NOT_A_SHAREHOLDER`. One dispute is allowed per market. Another wallet receives `DUPLICATE_DISPUTE` with no details about the first disputant.

`side` must oppose the oracle. Send `no` to dispute a YES proposal, and `yes` to dispute a NO proposal.

`pk_test_` keys receive sandbox fixtures and a `disclaimer` field. They do not touch mainnet.

Reason and evidence stay off-chain. They are stored for reviewers and are not placed in the transaction.
